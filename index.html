The Name of the Group

A closed working group devoted to psychoanalysis, language, literature, film, and the clinical questions that animate analytic thought.

We closely read Freud, Lacan, and other primary psychoanalytic texts, alongside works in philosophy, linguistics, literature, film, and critical theory. Our aim is not simply to produce psychoanalytic readings of art or culture, but to let these neighboring fields help us understand what psychoanalysis teaches about language, desire, ethics, and the speaking subject.

The group welcomes serious readers both inside and outside clinical practice. It is not a training program or supervision group, and it does not treat psychoanalysis as a decorative vocabulary for cultural commentary. Our work is grounded in texts, but remains oriented by the clinical problems from which psychoanalysis draws its force.
