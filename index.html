The Name of the Group

An independent reading group devoted to the study of Freudian and Lacanian psychoanalysis.

We focus on the writings of Freud and Lacan, supplemented occasionally by texts in philosophy, linguistics, literature, and other disciplines insofar as they illuminate the psychoanalytic questions at hand. We also periodically screen films as occasions for discussion. Our interest is not in applying psychoanalysis to diagnose art or culture, but in deepening our understanding of psychoanalysis itself.

The group welcomes anyone interested in reading and discussing psychoanalysis, whether they are students, clinicians, or simply curious. As the group is neither a training program nor a supervision group, our work is grounded in the texts themselves while remaining attentive to the clinical questions from which psychoanalysis emerged.

If you are interested in becoming a part of The Name of the Group, please note that extensive knowledge of Freud and Lacan is not required or expected; a willingness to read and discuss the texts is. Please reach out and introduce yourself to learn more about how we work.
