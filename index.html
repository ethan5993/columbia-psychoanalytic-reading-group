The Name of the Group

Columbia, South Carolina

The Name of the Group is a closed working group in Columbia, South Carolina devoted to the study of psychoanalysis, language, literature, and film.

We closely read Freud, Lacan, and other primary psychoanalytic texts, alongside works in philosophy, linguistics, literature, film, and critical theory. Our aim is not simply to produce psychoanalytic readings of art or culture, but to let these neighboring fields help us understand what psychoanalysis teaches about speech, desire, symptoms, ethics, and the speaking subject.

The group welcomes serious readers both inside and outside clinical practice. It is not a training program or supervision group, and it does not treat psychoanalysis as a decorative vocabulary for cultural commentary. Our work is grounded in texts, but remains oriented by the clinical problems from which psychoanalysis draws its force. force.
