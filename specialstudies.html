Special Studies

Special Studies are occasional, intensive gatherings devoted to particular questions, texts, and thinkers in psychoanalysis and related disciplines. Open to the public, each study offers an opportunity for collective reading and discussion without an ongoing commitment. Studies are proposed and organized by members of The Name of the Group.

The Dream Is Not a Story

Freud, Lacan, and Surrealism

What does it mean to interpret a dream? Is a dream a story concealing another story, or does its formation obey a different logic altogether?

This day-long study will explore Freud's theory of dream-work, Lacan's account of the unconscious and language, and the surrealist engagement with dreams and the irrational. Through collective reading, discussion, and selected films, we will consider the relationship between dreams, representation, desire, and interpretation.

Attendance: Open to the public. No prior familiarity with psychoanalysis is required. Those interested in participating may contact ethan.taylor@venueclinic.com . Readings, location, and further details will be provided in advance.
